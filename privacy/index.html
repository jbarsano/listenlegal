Privacy

Effective November 23, 2026

These tools are built so your information never leaves your device. Every calculation runs in your web browser, so nothing you type is sent anywhere. When you print or share, your own device handles it. You decide what gets saved, printed or shared.

Income Screening Tool. When you share a result, your income and household size are included in the link. Anyone with the link sees those figures. Your entries clear automatically when you leave the page.

Billable Hours Calculator. This tool remembers what you’ve entered. If you close the tab, shut the browser or restart your computer, your entries are still there when you come back. That way you don’t lose your time by accident. Your entries are saved only in your browser, on your device. They stay until you click Clear. On a shared computer, click Clear when you’re done.

Analytics and hosting. Two outside services help run the site. Google Analytics counts which tools and features people use, like printing or sharing. This informs which tools are most useful and what to improve next. It never sees anything you type. Like most websites, it uses cookies and gets basic details from your browser, such as device type and rough location. It does this even if your browser asks sites not to track you (a Do Not Track signal). GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it keeps basic visit records, such as IP address and browser type. Both follow their own privacy policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

For organizations reviewing these tools. Google Analytics is the only outside code the tools load. The tools still work fully if your organization blocks it. Google Analytics keeps usage data for 2 months. Advertising features are turned off. It does not store IP addresses. The code is public on GitHub, so your IT staff can review exactly what the tools do. The site uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
